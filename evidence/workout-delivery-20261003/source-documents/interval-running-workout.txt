RUNNING WORKOUT

Are you ready to take your running to the next level? High-Intensity Interval Running (HIIR) could be just the challenge you're looking for. HIIR alternates between bursts of intense effort and brief recovery periods, delivering maximum results in a shorter time.

Start the workout with a dynamic full-body warm-up.
Ankle circles, calves stretch, leg swings forward and to the sides, hip openers, 90-90s, high knee run, buttkicks, feet kick outs forward and backward, deep controlled squats, wall sit, shoulder circles, arm swings vertical and horizontal, elbow circles, wrists circles, neck twists and turn, torso bends, torso circles, torso twist, floor touches, cossack squats.

The main part of the workout includes 3-6 rounds of interval running where instead of rest you jog at a slow pace:

Round 1: 3 minutes of running at a fast pace
Rest: 1 minute of light jogging

Round 2: 3 minutes of running at a fast pace
Rest: 1 minute of light jogging

Round 3: 3 minutes of running at a fast pace
Rest: 1 minute of light jogging

Round 4: 3 minutes of running at a fast pace
Rest: 1 minute of light jogging

Round 5: 3 minutes of running at a fast pace
RestT: 1 minute of light jogging

Round 6: 3 minutes of running at a fast pace


Rest:
3-4 minutes of walking


The second part of the workout includes 3 rounds of sprint:

Round 1: 100-meter sprint
Rest: 1.5 - 2 minutes of walking

Round 2: 100-meter sprint
Res: 1.5 - 2 minutes of walking

Round 3: 100-meter sprint


Rest: 3-4 minutes of walking


The final part of the workout consists of 8-15 meter shuttles. Run 8 meters forward, then turn around and run 8 meters back, then turn and run 15 meters forward, turn around and run 15 meters back. That would be one 8-15 meter shuttle:

Round 1: Perform 8-15 meter shuttles (as described above)
Resr: 1 minute of walking

Round 2: Perform 8-15 meter shuttles (as described above)
Rest: 1 minute of walking

Round 3: Perform 8-15 meter shuttles (as described above)


Rest: 3-4 minutes of walking

Static stretches: finish off your workout with a series of static stretches to help your muscles recover and prevent any tightness.
Wide fold, calves stretch, quad stretch, knee over toes lunge position, side lunge hold.


By following this comprehensive running workout plan, you'll not only boost your endurance and speed but also enhance your overall fitness level. Remember to listen to your body and adjust the intensity as needed. Keep challenging yourself and enjoy the progress you'll make!

Remember, consistency is key to achieving your fitness goals. Keep pushing yourself and stay committed to your training routine. Happy running!
