# Coin visual audit — 26 September 2026

This is a fresh Ray of Light style review of the **running iPhone 11 simulator app**. I walked the French and English journey with the simulator UI, captured each settled state with `simctl io screenshot`, and made 27 screen-bound normalized rectangle annotations across 14 screenshots. The annotations and 10 clustered failure modes are in `annotations.json` and `patterns.json`; `samples.json` orders the journey. Open `index.html` through the bundled `app.py` to inspect rectangles on the exact captures. This replaces the earlier partial `design-review/` evidence; its film and calendar shots were stale.

The walk was: Sessions entry → English warm-up → skip four warm-up activities → boxing round → finish → immediate recap → session log → film notebook → connection settings → calendar → return to Sessions and select French → warm-up → skip four warm-up activities → boxing round → finish → recap → film notebook → calendar. Simulator camera was unavailable, so the black preview verifies layout and timer controls only. It does **not** verify camera composition, pose alignment, live exchange recognition, or cue latency over real footage.

## Priority fixes

1. **P0: Repair film notebook contrast.** Both [English](shots/07-film-en.png) and [French](shots/13-film-fr.png) show white title, subtitle and section labels on warm ivory. Some content is effectively invisible. Use dark ink on ivory or return the whole notebook to noir; apply the same token to status/nav text. Keep the existing stripped notebook structure. See `CoinApp.swift` `HomeView`.
2. **P0: Repair French recap metric layout.** In [French recap](shots/12-recap-fr.png), `ÉCHANTILLONS CAMÉRA` collides with `CONSIGNES DEMANDÉES`. Three equal columns are too narrow at iPhone 11 width. Stack or use two columns plus a full-width third metric; verify longer strings and Dynamic Type. See `TrainingViews.swift` `WorkoutRecapView`.
3. **P1: Stop bottom navigation from covering content.** [Sessions](shots/01-home-fr.png), [film](shots/13-film-fr.png), and [calendar](shots/14-calendar-fr.png) all scroll behind a floating tab bar with no clear content inset. The log and film-card rows are visibly covered. Add safe-area-aware bottom scroll clearance or integrate tabs into the layout; test the last row can be read and tapped.
4. **P1: Reduce display scale and keep workout evidence in view.** [Entry](shots/02-home-en.png) spends much of its initial viewport on an oversized headline and prose before the camera action. [English recap](shots/05-recap-en.png) and especially [French recap](shots/12-recap-fr.png) spend the top half on a title and summary. Shrink headlines and vertical gaps; lead recap with time actually trained and the first round record. Preserve the small original corner-post artwork at entry, not a generic dots mark.
5. **P1: Finish French locale propagation.** [French live round](shots/11-live-round-fr.png) still says `ROUND 1`; [French calendar](shots/14-calendar-fr.png) has `Sep` pills and `rounds` rows; [French recap](shots/12-recap-fr.png) mixes `rounds` and `drill` into otherwise French prose. Some loanwords may be a deliberate editorial choice, but the current mixed register needs one decision. `TrainingModel.swift` has explicit French `"%d rounds"` keys; the date formatter also needs the selected locale. Saved video titles are user/fixture data; label fixture content as demo rather than silently translating a stored title.
6. **P1: Compact the live HUD, then test with real camera.** [Warm-up](shots/10-live-warmup-fr.png) and [round](shots/11-live-round-fr.png) keep the center of the preview clear, but the timer and full-width red Start/Pause bar are still outsized at the bottom. Use one small current cue, a glanceable timer, and bounded controls. Do not claim a real-image safe area until tested with floor, side and bag placements on the iPhone.
7. **P1: Make round accounting read naturally.** [Recap](shots/05-recap-en.png) says `0 timed rounds` while immediately below it shows `ROUND 1` reached with ten seconds. The data may be technically correct (zero complete rounds), but users will read it as conflicting. Lead with `00:17 trained`, `1 round reached`, and separately say `0 full rounds completed`. Keep timer facts separate from drill-performance claims.
8. **P2: Align secondary surfaces with the same visual system.** [Session log](shots/06-session-log-en.png) and [calendar](shots/09-calendar-en.png) use oversized native grouped cards and show future planned rows at the same visual weight as actual workout evidence. Collapse untouched segments and make the completed activity visible first. [Connection settings](shots/08-settings-en.png) need less first-use prominence; the server URL, key, and pose sharing details can remain accessible without dominating the film path.

## Color and spacing diagnosis

The entry and live views establish near-black, cream text, gold for labels/timing, and vermilion for the primary action. The film view reverses to ivory but retains white text in the header, so the palette is internally inconsistent rather than simply stylistically different. The native Forms use gray cards, white headings, and large rounded corners; that is a third visual grammar. One token set should specify background, ink, muted ink, surface, hairline, gold, and red for both dark and light contexts, with contrast checked on screenshots.

The main sizing error is a repeated hierarchy error: every destination has a title-card scale even when the user needs an immediate action or evidence. The camera view is the product center. Entry should point quickly to camera; live should leave the frame clear; recap should put observed round evidence before decorative heading; log and calendar should be compact reference surfaces.

## Reproduction and limits

The simulator was `Coin iPhone 11` on iOS 26.5, booted device `CB5D7882-F35E-469E-980B-5729D3C0F7D4`. Captures are 828 × 1792 pixels. I navigated the installed app in the simulator, selected both languages, started timer-led sessions, skipped warm-up blocks to reach boxing, and finished early. This is a deterministic journey review of current UI state, not a scripted XCTest regression suite. Subsequent app edits require recapture and annotation comparison rather than treating this atlas as proof that fixes landed.

The review UI is copied from the [Ray of Light design-review tooling](https://github.com/ChaiWithJai/ray-of-light/tree/main/tools/design-review). To inspect locally, run `python3 app.py` from this folder and open `http://127.0.0.1:8377/`; the first view defaults to Mobile. The JSON files are source of truth for the annotations and clusters. No real camera frames, private footage, credentials, or model output are in this atlas.
